How it works

Three steps, nothing left behind

  1. Agree on a secret codeEnter your friend’s User ID and a code the two of you have agreed on. When you’ve both entered it, the chat opens.
  2. Chat privatelyMessages and photos are encrypted on your phone before they are sent. Only your friend’s phone can open them.
  3. Chat ends, messages goWhen the chat ends, the messages are cleared from your phone. The server never stored them.

Delete your account

You can do it yourself, inside the app, in under a minute. There is nothing to request by email and nobody to wait for.

Last updated: November 2026

Step by step

How to delete your account

  1. Log inOpen the Surakshit app and log in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open your detailsGo to Settings, then open Personal Information.
  3. DeleteTap Delete Account, then confirm.
This cannot be undone.

Once you confirm, there is no way to restore the account, its User ID or anything connected to it.

What happens next

What is deleted, and when

Right away

  • You are signed out and can no longer log in to the account.
  • The account is deactivated, and nobody can start a new chat with it.
  • Your sign-in record is removed.

After 30 days

  • The account is permanently deleted: User ID, password hash and activity dates.
  • People you blocked, and people who blocked you, are removed from the block lists.

Nothing to delete

Messages and photos are never stored on our servers, and on your phone they exist only while a chat is open.

What we keep

  • Chat records: that a chat took place between two accounts, with its start and end times. No messages. After the account is permanently deleted, it cannot be tied to any User ID.
  • Reports: reports you sent, and reports about your account, are kept so that repeated abuse can be recognised. They hold no messages.

The Privacy Policy explains each of these in full.

Can’t log in?

If the app isn’t an option

I lost my phone or forgot my password

You cannot delete the account from the app, and we cannot recover a password, because we hold no email address or phone number for you. We also usually cannot confirm that an account is yours.

You do not have to do anything, though. An account that is not used for 14 days is deactivated automatically, and 30 days after that it is permanently deleted.

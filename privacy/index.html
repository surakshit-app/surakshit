Privacy Policy

What we collect, what we don’t, and how long we keep it — in plain words.

Last updated: November 2026

Messages aren’t stored

They’re encrypted on your phone before they are sent, and the server only passes them along.

No phone number or email

We don’t ask for your phone number, email address, location or contacts.

No ads, no tracking

There is no advertising, no tracking and no analytics in the app, and we do not sell anything.

Delete any time

You can delete your account yourself, inside the app. Here is how.

01What we collect and keep

This is everything the Surakshit servers hold about you:

  • Your User ID, so you can sign in and a friend can reach you.
  • Your password, in scrambled form. It is stored only as a one-way hash. We cannot read it and we cannot reset it.
  • When you last logged in and last used the app, so that unused accounts can be removed.
  • A sign-in record. A random code, kept for up to 30 days or until you log out, that lets the server tell that your phone is the one signed in to your account.
  • A record of each chat. It shows that a chat took place between two accounts, when it started, and when and how it ended. It never includes any messages. It refers to accounts by internal identifiers, not by User ID.
  • The people you block. We keep the pair (you and the person you blocked) so the block works even if you change phones.
  • Reports you send. A report records who was reported, which chat it was, the reason you chose, the note you wrote (if any), and when. A report never includes messages, because we do not have any.
  • Short-lived counters. To slow down password guessing and flooding, the server briefly counts requests by network address and by account. These counters expire within an hour and are not kept in our logs.

If you write to our support email, we will see the address you write from, and we keep your message to answer it.

02What we don’t collect

  • Your phone number, email address, real name, location or contacts.
  • The content of your messages or photos.
  • An advertising ID. The app has no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.

03How messages and photos work

  • Messages and photos are encrypted on your phone before they are sent. Only the phone of the person you are chatting with can open them.
  • The server passes the encrypted data along without storing it.
  • On your phone, a chat is kept only in the app’s memory while it is open. When the chat ends or the app is closed, it is gone.
  • The app has no way to save photos from a chat. On Android, screenshots and screen recording are blocked inside the app. On iPhone the app cannot block screenshots, so it shows a warning when one is taken.

04Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera, only when you choose to take a photo to send in a chat.

To send a picture from your phone, the app uses your phone’s own photo picker. You choose one photo and the app receives only that photo; it never gets access to your photo library, so it asks for no photo permission. The app does not use your microphone, location or contacts. A photo you send is encrypted before it leaves your phone.

05What stays on your phone

Your app settings, including an optional app passcode if you set one, are stored on your phone only. They are never sent to us.

06App updates

The app can download small updates over the air from Expo’s update service. To deliver an update, that service receives the technical details any download involves, such as your network address, the app version and your device type. No account information is sent with it.

07Who we share information with

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. We use hosting and database providers to run the service, and they handle data on our behalf; they may keep their own standard server logs. We may also disclose information if the law requires it. Because we hold so little, there is very little to disclose.

08Reports, blocking and suspensions

You can report or block the other person from the menu in a chat. A person reads reports; nothing happens to an account automatically. If someone is found to have broken the Terms & Conditions, we may suspend their account. A suspended account cannot sign in, and we keep its User ID and a note of the suspension so that the ID cannot be registered again.

09How long we keep things

WhatHow long
Account (User ID, password hash, activity dates)Until you delete it, or until it goes unused. An account unused for 14 days is deactivated, and 30 days after that it is permanently deleted. Using the app keeps it active.

10Your choices

  • Delete your account in the app, under Settings → Personal Information. See what happens when you do.
  • Unblock people under Settings → Blocked users.
  • Ask us a question. Because we hold no email or phone number for you, we may not be able to confirm that an account is yours. We will do what we reasonably can.

11Security

Messages are end-to-end encrypted, passwords are stored only as hashes, and the app talks to our servers over an encrypted connection. No system is perfectly secure, so please choose a strong password and keep your passcode private.

12Children

Surakshit is for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly allow anyone younger to use it. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, write to us and we will look into it.

13Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The date at the top shows when it last changed. If a change affects what we collect, we will say so in the app.

14Contact

Questions about privacy?
